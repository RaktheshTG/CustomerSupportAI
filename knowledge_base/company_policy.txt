CloudNest — Company Policies

ABOUT CLOUDNEST
CloudNest is a software company that provides a secure file-sync and team
collaboration application. Customers use CloudNest to store files, sync them
across devices, and share folders with teammates. These policies apply to all
CloudNest plans (Basic, Pro, and Enterprise).

SUPPORT HOURS AND RESPONSE TIME
- Automated AI support (chat and email) is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a
  week.
- Human support agents are available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  (customer's local time).
- CloudNest guarantees a first response to every support request within 24
  hours.

REFUND POLICY
- Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of their purchase or
  renewal date. This is the "30-day refund window."
- Refunds requested within the 30-day window are approved in full.
- After 30 days, refunds are not provided, except when CloudNest has a
  documented service outage that affected the customer. In that case a partial
  or full refund may be granted at CloudNest's discretion.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business
  days.
- Refunds over $100 require review by a human support manager before they are
  issued.

CANCELLATION POLICY
- Customers may cancel their subscription at any time.
- After cancellation, the account remains active until the end of the current
  paid billing period. No further charges are made after that.
- Cancelling does not automatically delete stored files. Files remain available
  until the end of the paid period and are then archived for 30 days before
  deletion.
- Because cancellation ends a paid service, every cancellation request is
  reviewed by a human agent before it is finalised.

PLAN CHANGES
- Upgrades (for example Basic to Pro) take effect immediately. The customer is
  charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, so the customer
  keeps their current features until then.

PRIVACY AND DATA
- CloudNest encrypts all stored files at rest and in transit.
- CloudNest never sells customer data.
- Customers may request export or deletion of their data by contacting support.
- Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days of the request.

ACCEPTABLE USE
- CloudNest may not be used to store or share illegal content.
- Accounts found violating the acceptable use policy may be suspended after a
  warning.

ESCALATION
- If the AI assistant cannot answer a question reliably, or a customer is
  dissatisfied, the request is escalated to a human support agent and a support
  ticket is created. The customer is told a human will follow up within 24
  hours.
