CloudNest — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is CloudNest?
A: CloudNest is a secure file-sync and team collaboration app. It stores your
   files, syncs them across your devices, and lets you share folders with
   teammates.

Q: How much does CloudNest cost?
A: There are three plans: Basic at $9/month, Pro at $29/month, and Enterprise at
   $99/month. Annual billing saves 20%. See the pricing guide for details.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes, every plan has a 14-day free trial. You are not charged until the trial
   ends.

Q: How do I get a refund?
A: You can request a full refund within 30 days of a charge. Refunds are
   returned to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
   Refunds over $100 are reviewed by a support manager first.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can cancel any time. Your account stays active until the end of the
   current paid period, and no further charges are made. A human agent reviews
   every cancellation before it is finalised.

Q: How many devices can I use?
A: Basic supports 2 devices. Pro and Enterprise support unlimited devices.

Q: How much storage do I get?
A: Basic includes 100 GB, Pro includes 1 TB, and Enterprise includes unlimited
   storage.

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Yes. CloudNest encrypts files both at rest and in transit, and never sells
   customer data.

Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?
A: Use "Forgot password" on the login screen. A reset link is emailed to you and
   is valid for 1 hour.

Q: Why are my files not syncing?
A: The most common reasons are no internet connection, being over your storage
   limit, or a file that is too large for your plan. See the technical manual
   for full steps.

Q: Can I share files with people who don't have CloudNest?
A: Yes. You can create a share link that anyone can open, even without a
   CloudNest account.

Q: How do I contact a human?
A: If the assistant cannot help, it will create a support ticket and a human
   agent will follow up within 24 hours. Human agents work Monday to Friday,
   9 AM to 6 PM.

Q: What operating systems are supported?
A: CloudNest works on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
