CloudNest — Pricing Guide

CloudNest offers three subscription plans. All prices are in US dollars and are
billed per month. Annual billing is available at a 20% discount (pay for 10
months, get 12).

------------------------------------------------------------
BASIC PLAN — $9 per month
------------------------------------------------------------
Best for: individuals.
- 100 GB of secure storage
- Sync across up to 2 devices
- File sharing by link
- Email support (24-hour response)
- 30-day file version history

------------------------------------------------------------
PRO PLAN — $29 per month
------------------------------------------------------------
Best for: small teams (up to 10 people).
- 1 TB of secure storage
- Sync across unlimited devices
- Shared team folders
- Advanced permissions (view / edit / admin)
- Priority email and chat support
- 180-day file version history

------------------------------------------------------------
ENTERPRISE PLAN — $99 per month
------------------------------------------------------------
Best for: large organisations.
- Unlimited storage
- Unlimited devices and users
- Single sign-on (SSO) and admin console
- Audit logs and compliance reports
- Dedicated account manager
- Phone, chat, and email support
- 1-year file version history

------------------------------------------------------------
BILLING RULES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Subscriptions renew automatically each month on the purchase date.
- Payment methods: major credit cards and PayPal.
- Upgrades are charged immediately and prorated for the current cycle.
- Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
- A full refund is available within 30 days of any charge (see the company
  policy document for the full refund policy).
- Refunds over $100 require human manager approval before processing.
- Failed payments are retried for 7 days; after that the account is paused until
  payment succeeds.

------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON PRICING QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- "Can I try CloudNest for free?" Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial.
  No charge is made until the trial ends.
- "What happens to my files if I downgrade?" Files are kept. If you exceed the
  storage limit of the lower plan, you can still download files but cannot
  upload new ones until you are under the limit.
- "Do you offer discounts?" Annual billing saves 20%. Enterprise customers can
  contact sales for volume pricing.
